Remove or correct your details

If your phone number, company or other details appear on Giboro and you want them removed or corrected, email us with:

  • the link to the page, or the permit number, where you saw them;
  • what you want removed or corrected.

We act within 10 business days, usually sooner. A removed phone number is hidden everywhere on Giboro. The record itself stays with the government agency that published it; to change that, contact the agency.

Your account and data

To see, correct or delete your saved permits, notes, digest settings or usage records, email us from, or naming, the address you signed in with. See Privacy for what we hold.

Emails
