What Giboro is

Giboro is a search tool over building-permit and planning records that government agencies publish, with some public property, licence and business records added. It helps businesses find construction work and the companies doing it. The records come from the agencies listed on Data sources.

The data is as published

We show records the way the agencies publish them. They can be incomplete, late or wrong, and we can't guarantee any of them. Check the issuing agency's own record before you rely on it for a bid, a contract or any other decision. The Disclaimer explains the main limits. Giboro is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency.
