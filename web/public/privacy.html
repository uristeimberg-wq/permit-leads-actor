Privacy and usage analytics

Contractos records how the app is used so we can learn which data and screens are useful. This page says what is recorded, what is not, and how to turn it off.

What we record

  • Pages you open and how long the tab was visible on each (time in a background tab is not counted), and how far you scroll.
  • Searches: the words you type in the search box, the filters you choose, and how many results came back.
  • Which permits, projects and contractors you open, and actions such as saving a permit, copying or calling a phone number, or opening a county portal link.
  • Errors the app hit, so we can fix them.
  • A random identifier stored in your browser (localStorage), so visits from the same browser can be counted as one visitor. If you sign in with your email to save permits, that browser's earlier visits are linked to your email.
  • The website that sent you here (its name only, not the page address) and campaign tags in the link, if any.
  • Device type (phone, tablet, desktop), browser and operating system family.

What we do not record

  • Your IP address. The analytics data never holds it; it is only used, in memory, to slow down abuse while a request is handled.
  • Your full browser identification string, passwords, keys or cookies.
  • Email addresses or phone numbers typed into the search box: they are replaced with a placeholder before anything is stored.
  • The phone numbers you copy or call: only that you used one.

How long we keep it

Usage records are deleted automatically 13 months after they were recorded.

The data stays in our own database. It is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and no third-party analytics or advertising service is used. Only the site owner can see it.

Turning it off

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is recorded. You can also switch it off for this browser here.
